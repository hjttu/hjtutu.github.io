I am Jiatao Hu, an undergraduate student in Automation at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics. I will join the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in September 2027.

My research focuses on multi-agent reinforcement learning and cooperative decision-making. I am particularly interested in how agents allocate tasks, coordinate around moving targets, and communicate when interaction risks arise.

## Research

- [Heterogeneous multi-agent task allocation]({{ '/projects/heterogeneous-task-allocation/' | relative_url }}): coordinating teams with different roles under tight task and execution constraints.
- [Dynamic target capture]({{ '/projects/dynamic-target-capture/' | relative_url }}): helping robot teams coordinate the capture of multiple moving targets.
- [Predictive risk-triggered communication]({{ '/projects/predictive-risk-communication/' | relative_url }}): exploring when agents should communicate to support coordinated motion in crowded environments.

See the [research projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) for short summaries and simulation demos.
