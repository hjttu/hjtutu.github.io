This project studies how agents with different roles can form teams and complete tasks when timing, team composition, and movement constraints interact. The work connects task allocation with team coordination and execution feedback.

The associated paper has been [accepted by IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVT.2026.3701968).
